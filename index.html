DOLAN'S SHELTER

HOW TO PLAY

Exhibition is one game. Season is 8 games, then a playoff and the Insura-Bowl if you win 4 or more.

Call a play, then hike. On offense the D-pad moves the ball carrier. A throws to the highlighted receiver, or dives and stiff-arms once you're running. B cycles receivers, then jukes. On defense, A dives and B switches to the nearest tackler.

Field goals are a timing bar. Tap A when the needle is in the green. Short quarters, lots of points. Bombs, crunches, pick-sixes, and a last-second Hail Claim all get a banner.

Pat Boom and Kenny South call the game out loud and on the ticker under the score. They roast picks, missed kicks, and blowouts. The ticker stays put when the play-call sheet is up. Win a rivalry or the Insura-Bowl and you lift the trophy.

Desktop: arrows or WASD move, Z is A, X is B, Enter hikes. Keys 1–6 pick a play.
