# Design Bible — Dolan's Shelter Insura-Bowl

## Pillars
- Classic Tecmo Bowl pixel look, phone browser first.
- Scoring bias: fast drives, big plays, short quarters, high scores.
- Every game feels like a highlight reel: music + SFX for TDs, catches, and bone-crunching tackles.

## Season format
8-game regular season → 2 playoff games → **Insura-Bowl** championship.

## Big moments
- **BOMB** — deep pass TD with slow-mo catch.
- **CRUNCH** — huge tackle, screen shake, crunch SFX.
- **PICK-SIX** — interception returned for TD.
- **HAIL CLAIM** — last-second Hail Mary ("file a claim!").

## Announcers
- **Pat Boom** — John Madden-style booming play-by-play ("BOOM! Now there's a guy who…").
- **Kenny South** — South Park-style goofy, irreverent color commentary.
- **Rule: when the player does poorly, they get MOCKED.** No soft encouragement when losing — Pat and Kenny roast you (stingy, funny, Madden boom + South Park snark) so you get mad and want to do better.

### Taunt / mock bank
- **Turnover:** Pat: "BOOM! And that's the sound of a football leaving a guy who clearly didn't want it." / Kenny: "Dude, you just gave it away like free samples at Costco."
- **Blown coverage:** Pat: "Nobody's within twenty yards of that receiver. You could land a plane back there!" / Kenny: "Was your safety on a smoke break? Seriously?"
- **Missed FG:** Pat: "That kick had about as much direction as my uncle at a buffet." / Kenny: "Wide left, wide dumb. Screw you guys, I'm going home."
- **3-and-out streak:** Pat: "Three plays, three yards, three reasons to change the channel." / Kenny: "Your offense has the same energy as a Tuesday sub."
- **Blowout deficit:** Pat: "When you're down this much, you start thinking about your insurance deductible." / Kenny: "Oh my God, they killed your season! You bastards!"
- **Dropped pass:** Pat: "Right in the hands! Right OUT of the hands! Hands of a guy holding a hot pizza." / Kenny: "Butterfingers! Did you butter them on purpose?"

### Insurance joke bank (pepper everywhere)
**Touchdowns**
- Pat: "BOOM! Touchdown! That's comprehensive coverage — covered every blade of grass on the way in!"
- Kenny: "Claim APPROVED, baby! No adjuster needed for that one."
- Pat: "He hit that end zone like a guy who just found out his premium went DOWN."

**Big bombs**
- Pat: "That ball's got an umbrella policy — it's covered from sixty yards out!"
- Kenny: "That's not a pass, that's an act of God. Totally not covered by the defense."
- Pat: "The underwriter looked at that throw and said 'nope, too risky' — and he threw it anyway! BOOM!"

**Sacks (mocking the player when you get sacked)**
- Pat: "Collision coverage! And your quarterback just used ALL of it."
- Kenny: "Dude, your O-line has zero liability coverage. They just let him walk in."
- Pat: "That's a full-on total loss. The adjuster's gonna need dental records."

**Interceptions (thrown by the player)**

- Kenny: "You just voided your warranty on that offense, dude."

**Missed field goals**

**Player crushing it (celebration)**

### Commentary rules
1. **Iterate / rotate — no stale repeats.** Lines come from a large bank tagged by situation (TD, sack, INT, missed FG, 3-and-out, blowout, comeback, big bomb, crunch). Pick by situation weight; a line can't repeat until most of its bucket has been used, with per-game and per-season cooldowns. Keep growing the bank.
2. **Football internet humor energy.** Roast culture, meme-style gags (Dez-nuts-style setup/punchline bits), Madden BOOM, South Park deadpan. Keep the insurance gags and the mock-on-poor-play rule. Keep it parody: no real trademarked catchphrases, no real insurer slogans.
3. **Funny beats bland.** The wit and humor are what keep the game replayable. When in doubt, cut the generic play-by-play and use the joke.

## Playable build

The game is a static site. GitHub Pages: https://antonolson47-ctrl.github.io/dolans-shelter-insura-bowl/

Open `index.html` through a local server (`python3 -m http.server`). Phone: on-screen D-pad, **A** (throw / dive / kick), **B** (cycle receiver / juke / switch defender). Desktop: arrows or WASD, Z, X, Enter, keys 1–6. Exhibition is one game. Season is 8 games, a playoff, and the Insura-Bowl if you win at least 4. Quarters are 3:00. Rivalry wins and the Insura-Bowl open the trophy lift (`art/insura-bowl-trophy.jpg`, `art/rivalry-trophies.jpg`). Cover and crest for sharing: `art/cover.jpg`, `art/crest.jpg`.
