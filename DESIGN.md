# Design Bible — Dolan's Shelter Insura-Bowl

## Pillars
- Classic Tecmo Bowl pixel look, phone browser first.
- Scoring bias: fast drives, big plays, short quarters, high scores.
- Every game feels like a highlight reel: music + SFX for TDs, catches, and bone-crunching tackles.

## Playable teams (chosen at start)
| Team | Colors | Mascot / Logo | Helmet # |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iowa State Cyclodes | Cardinal & yellow | Tornado | 7 |
| New York Gi-ants | Royal blue & white | Insect ant | 11 |
| Lowell Shelter Dogs (local invent team) | Green & silver | Dog with CLAIM tag | 47 |

## Opponents (fictional)
- **Big 12–style conference** for the Cyclodes schedule (parody college programs).
- **NFC East–style division** for the Gi-ants schedule (parody pro teams: Dallas, Philly, Washington analogs).
- Shelter Dogs also appear in opponent lists.

## Season format
8-game regular season → 2 playoff games → **Insura-Bowl** championship.

## Big moments
- **BOMB** — deep pass TD with slow-mo catch.
- **CRUNCH** — huge tackle, screen shake, crunch SFX.
- **PICK-SIX** — interception returned for TD.
- **HAIL CLAIM** — last-second Hail Mary ("file a claim!").

## Announcers
- **Pat Boom** — John Madden-style booming play-by-play ("BOOM! Now there's a guy who…").
- **Kenny South** — South Park-style goofy, irreverent color commentary.

## Audio list
Title theme, team-select loop, game music, crowd loop, whistle, snap/hike, catch, TD fanfare, crunch tackle, pick-six sting, Hail Claim choir, field goal, fumble, halftime, playoff & Insura-Bowl themes, announcer one-liners.
